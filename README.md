# Morphology-Aware--VAE-framework-with-CNN-for-Material-Property-prediction-from-Microstructure-Images

Accurate prediction of material properties from microstructure images is important for advanced engineering applications; however, it remains challenging due to high-dimensional data representation, limited datasets, and complex process–structure–property relationships. To overcome these limitations, this paper introduced a novel morphology-aware β-Variational Autoencoder (β-VAE) framework integrated with a convolutional neural network (CNN) for efficient material property prediction. The proposed framework employs β-regularization to learn compact and disentangled latent representations of microstructures, enabling improved feature extraction and representation learning. To preserve important spatial and morphological characteristics, a morphology-aware style constraint based on deep feature representations is incorporated into the generative learning process. In addition, a diversity regularization mechanism is introduced to improve the variability and realism of generated microstructures while enhancing training stability. The generated synthetic samples are subsequently combined with authentic microstructure data to train a CNN-based predictive model for accurate material property estimation. Experimental evaluation conducted on the Micro2D Dataset demonstrates the effectiveness of the proposed framework, achieving an R^2 score of 0.953 and outperforming existing deep learning approaches for material property prediction. Furthermore, latent space visualization and convergence analysis confirm that the proposed framework learns compact, well-structured, and physically meaningful representations of heterogeneous microstructures.
